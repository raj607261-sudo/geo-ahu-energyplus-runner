How it works

Hybrid flow in four steps

Peak pull-down is separated from efficient long-duration holding.

High-ton pre-cool

Fresh warm produce gets rapid pull-down in the booster chamber.

Insulated transfer

The cooled batch moves through an interlocked transfer path.

Geo-AHU holding

A lower-ton ground-loop circuit handles the steady holding load.

Selected rooms stay stable

Only rooms that need cooling use the shared plant at one time.

Independent research · original papers

Three studies that support the Geo-AHU mechanism

Ground-source savings in Indian peak weather, water-cooled condenser savings, and a hybrid earth-and-water cooling architecture.

36.85%

electrical energy saved in peak cooling

IIT Roorkee · Experimental · 2024

Experimental Investigation of Energy-Saving Potential of Ground Source Heat Pump During Peak Hour Operations

Sah, Murugesan & Rajasekar · ASME Journal of Thermal Science and Engineering Applications

A 17.58 kW GSHP was tested against a 17.57 kW air-source system in Roorkee: 36.85% cooling-mode and 38.65% heating-mode electrical savings.

GEO-AHU RELEVANCEStrong Indian-climate evidence for the ground-source principle. It is a space-conditioning test, not a cold-storage certification.

21–27%

energy saving reported

Water-cooled condenser · 2017

Performance studies on domestic refrigerators retrofitted with building-integrated water-cooled condenser

P. Saji Raveendran & S. Joseph Sekhar · Energy and Buildings 134

Replacing an air-cooled condenser with a water-cooled one gave 21–27% energy savings; the brazed-plate option also reduced TEWI.

GEO-AHU RELEVANCEDirect evidence for the cooler-water condenser and BPHE principle. Domestic-refrigerator scale cannot be copied as a 10 MT guarantee.

14.0 °C

lowest optimised outlet-air temperature

Scientific Reports · Vol. 16 · 2026

Synergizing building-integrated photovoltaic with ground-air and water-air heat exchangers for solar-powered gym cooling

Ben Ali et al. · Scientific Reports 16, Article 2041

The optimised hybrid EAHE–WAHE series layout reached 14.0 °C outlet air; the parallel layout produced 41 kWh in August, 64% above the series configuration.

GEO-AHU RELEVANCESupports a hybrid earth-and-water pre-cooling architecture. It is a building-cooling study, not a refrigeration field trial.

Important: research-paper percentages belong to their own experimental systems. Geo-AHU's commercial saving will be claimed only after an instrumented side-by-side pilot in Bihar.
